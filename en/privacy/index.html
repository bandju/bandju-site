Privacy

Only anonymized technical data.

BandjuPanel uses only anonymized technical data that helps the app work correctly and improve over time.

What may be counted

Sometimes we record anonymized technical events so the app can stay reliable - for example, that a macOS or Windows version was downloaded and which app version is being used.

The app may also send a short anonymous activity signal once per day. It does not contain personal information and includes only basic technical details, such as device type, launcher version, and license type (Free or Pro).

What we do not collect

We do not access personal or sensitive information:

  • IP addresses in product statistics;
  • usernames and personal data;
  • VPS addresses and server lists;
  • VPN and proxy configurations;
  • personal files;
  • diagnostic logs are not sent automatically either - only if you decide to send them yourself.

Why this exists

A small amount of anonymized data helps us keep the app stable and improve it carefully over time.

This information is used only to understand how the app works in general. It does not involve your data, servers, or settings.
